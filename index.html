Section 01

GRAHAS — THE NINE PLANETS

The word Graha means "to seize" or "to grasp." These nine celestial forces influence all aspects of human experience — from the soul's purpose to the body's health.

Section 02

RASHIS — THE ZODIAC

The twelve signs of the zodiac form the fixed backdrop through which the planets move. Each Rashi represents a specific energy pattern, element, and modality of expression.

Section 03

BHAVAS — THE AREAS OF LIFE

The twelve houses (Bhavas) represent the twelve fundamental areas of human experience. Unlike Western astrology, in Jyotish the houses are fixed — the sign positions determine which energies inhabit each domain of life.

Section 04

NAKSHATRAS — THE LUNAR MANSIONS

The 27 (traditionally 28 with Abhijit) Nakshatras are lunar constellations spanning 13°20' each. They form the most ancient layer of Vedic astrology, deeply tied to the Moon's rhythm and the soul's subtle desires.

Section 05

KALA — THE TIME PRINCIPLES

Jyotish is fundamentally a science of time (Kala). The Panchanga and Dasha systems decode the qualitative nature of each moment and life period.

The Panchanga — Five Limbs of Time

Vimshottari Dasha System

The 120-year planetary period system based on the Moon's Nakshatra at birth. Each planet governs a specific number of years.

APPLICATION BRANCHES
